2026 · Machine Learning

Air Quality Classification

Clustering Analysis Lead

Compared K-Means, hierarchical clustering, and Gaussian mixture models on 9,358 hourly sensor records.

  • Evaluated clustering with Silhouette, Davies–Bouldin, ARI, and NMI.
  • Used a cluster-disjoint split to reduce leakage between similar samples.
  • The team achieved a Macro-F1 score of 0.8328.
